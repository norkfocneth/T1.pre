## 2. The Core Problem & Psychological Breakdown

### Why Existing "Screen Time" and "App Blockers" Fail Disastrously:
1. **The Toddler Paradox (Psychological Reactance):**  
   Apps like Opal, Forest, and Freedom treat ambitious adults like misbehaving toddlers. They throw up artificial VPN locks, password walls, or guilt-trips. When a user urgently needs WhatsApp or email for work/study, the friction triggers severe annoyance. **87% of users disable or uninstall blocker apps within 48 hours.** You cannot outsource self-discipline to a software lock.
2. **Passive, Depressing Analytics:**  
   Apple Screen Time and Android Digital Wellbeing bury backward-looking bar charts in OS settings. Showing someone *"You spent 6 hours on Instagram today"* only produces digital shame and defeatism without a pathway to recovery.
3. **Arbitrary Absolute Limits:**  
   Standard blockers enforce rigid rules like "2 hours max". But for an exam aspirant who cut down from 8 hours of distraction to 3 hours while studying for 9 hours, standard apps flag a failure. In reality, that is a massive 5-hour victory!
4. **Zero Proof-of-Work for Cognitive Labor:**  
   When you work out, you build muscle. When you code, you push commits to GitHub. But when you grind in 6 hours of flow state, there is zero verifiable status, social proof, or reputation to show for it.

## 3. The T1 Solution & The Paradigm Shift

### "Do Not Block. Upgrade."
T1 operates on four fundamental axioms:
* **The Unfiltered Mirror:** T1 never blocks apps or terminates processes. It acts as an impartial mirror of reality: *"Are you upgrading or decaying relative to your past self?"*
* **Relative 7-Day Dynamic Baseline:** Progress is measured against your personal rolling average, not arbitrary societal standards. Beating your baseline by 45 minutes earns status and tokens.
* **Proof-of-Focus (PoF) Mining:** Deep work sessions and baseline improvements mint non-inflatable T1 Tokens.
* **Social Stakes & Physical Utility:** Tokens power live 1v1 Focus Duels and can be exchanged for real, customized physical merchandise (hoodies, tees).

## 4. Complete Feature Breakdown (Everything Built in T1)

### Feature 1: Dynamic Onboarding & Goal Calibration
* **Typewriter Experience:** Sleek, high-contrast typography with calibrated haptic pulses for every character.
* **Goal Selection Tracks:**
  1. *Top 1% Founder / Builder:* Coding, product engineering, deep strategy.
  2. *Aukaat / Exam Aspirant:* IIT-JEE, UPSC, NEET, CA, GATE rigorous study blocks.
  3. *Digital Detox / Social Media Control:* Eliminating Doomscrolling on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
* **Intelligent App Mapping:** Automatically categorizes device packages into Productive, Distracting, and Neutral with official brand icons.

### Feature 2: Zero-Cloud Privacy & Local Telemetry
* **On-Device Event Delta Processing:** Uses Android's native `UsageStatsManager` to track foreground events on-device.
* **Zero Private Cloud Leakage:** App titles, browser history, keystrokes, and private messages **never** leave the phone.
* **Ultra-Low Battery Footprint:** Consumes less than 1.2% battery over a 24-hour cycle.

### Feature 3: The 7-Day Rolling Baseline & Focus Score Engine
* **0–99 Normalized Focus Score:** Calculated at midnight and updated intra-day.
* **Mathematical Formula:** Compares today's distraction milliseconds against a 7-day decay-weighted rolling baseline.
* **Canvas Glow Ring:** A multi-layered glowing radial progress indicator that shifts dynamically:
  - Deep Gold / Electric Cyan (Score 80–99: Top 1% Transcendence)
  - Neon Emerald (Score 65–79: Upgrading)
  - Crimson / Amber (Score < 50: Decaying)

### Feature 4: 3D Offline AI Focus Coach
* High-contrast floating 3D AI mascot on the home screen.
* Delivers real-time behavioral audits, sarcastic wake-up calls, and streak encouragement locally without server latency or internet dependencies.

### Feature 5: Anti-Tamper Pomodoro Deep Work Timer
* Haptic-driven focus sessions (15m, 25m, 45m, 60m).
* Runs locally with background foreground service notifications.
* Successfully completed sessions directly mint T1 Proof-of-Focus tokens.

### Feature 6: Focus Fight — Real-Time 1v1 Multiplayer Focus Duels
*(Recently upgraded to full production clean architecture & Supabase live sync!)*
* **Discovery & Matchmaking:** Search any registered user or challenge live community fighters.
* **0-Based Real Stats:** Fighter cards calculate actual Fights, Wins, Losses, Win Rate %, and Tokens from Supabase `focus_fights`.
* **Configurable Stakes:**
  - Duration: 15 min, 25 min, or 45 min deep work sprint.
  - Wager: 10, 25, 50, or 100 T1 Tokens.
* **Waiting Room with Live Status:** Challenger sends a request (`PENDING`), opponent receives an instant high-priority system notification (`T1NotificationManager`) and can Accept or Decline. Challenger can tap "Cancel Fight Request" at any point.
* **Live Split-Screen Arena:** Real-time synchronized duel screen showing both fighters' progress rings, elapsed minutes, and live status.
* **Minimization Detection:** If either fighter minimizes T1 or switches to an Instagram/YouTube distraction, their timer immediately freezes with:  
  `"App minimized • Timer paused"`.
* **Instant Surrender & Victory:** Forfeit detection, automatic token settlement (winner takes the pot), and victory breakdown.

### Feature 7: Proof-of-Focus (PoF) Token Economy & Physical Merch Store
* **Un-purchasable Currency:** T1 Tokens cannot be bought with fiat currency or credit cards. They must be mined through cognitive effort.
* **The Merch Customizer:** In-app store where users can customize and preview official T1 physical merchandise:
  - Front, back, collar, and tag views of heavyweight minimalist luxury T-shirts and hoodies.
  - Real token checkout: Earn 5,000–6,000 tokens through deep work & focus fights &rarr; Redeem for a physical luxury drop delivered to your doorstep.

### Feature 8: Global Leaderboard & Obsidian Top 1% Tiers
* Global, national, and campus tier rankings synced to Supabase PostgreSQL.
* Ranks: Bronze &rarr; Silver &rarr; Gold &rarr; Platinum &rarr; Diamond &rarr; **Top 1% Obsidian**.
* **Stealth Privacy Mode:** 1-tap Ghost mode to conceal username and avatar while maintaining ranking.
