# T1 Reel Storylines (9:16 Vertical Video · 20–25s)

## Recommendation: Storyline 1 ("The Late-Night Loop to Summit Peak")
**Why:** It connects directly with the relatable pain of nighttime phone doomscrolling that everyone experiences, showcases our high-fidelity 3D mascot's emotional arc from struggle to triumph, and leverages the exact visual assets already perfected in the app.

---

### Storyline 1: "The Late-Night Loop to Summit Peak" (Recommended)
- **Device & Form:** A character does the work / Match cuts and continuous vertical climb
- **What the viewer sees & feels:** We see our cute astronaut mascot trapped in the hypnotic purple glow of doomscrolling at 2:00 AM, sinking deeper into regret. Then T1's focus shield engages—the mascot puts on headphones, enters intense focus flow, locks out distractions, and climbs all the way to a peaceful neon mountain summit holding the T1 flag with 100% daily discipline achieved. The viewer feels the painful familiarity of phone addiction shift into triumphant dopamine and calm control.
- **First 2 seconds:** Mascot curled in bed in pitch black, phone screen casting a harsh strobe on tired glowing eyes, a digital clock flashing `02:14 AM` and a giant notification bubble: *"Just 5 more minutes..."*
- **Beats:** Hook (Late night doomscroll trap) → Tension (Time slipping away, screen time hits 6h+) → Reveal (T1 activates: "Break the loop") → Proof (Focus session timer locks distractions + cute mascot working with laser focus) → Payoff (Summit celebration under the moon, discipline score 100%) → End Card (T1 mark + "Reclaim your focus. Download T1.").
- **Look:** Deep obsidian purple background (`#0B0814`), glowing neon violet accents (`#A855F7`), clean bold typography (Outfit / Inter), glassmorphic HUD cards with neon rim light.
- **Sound:** Bass-heavy muffled notification pings fading into an upbeat lo-fi synthwave drop, satisfying focus lock click, and ambient mountain wind chime at the summit.

---

### Storyline 2: "The 1v1 Boss Fight Against Distraction"
- **Device & Form:** The feature as the hook (Focus Battle mode) / Cut on the beat
- **What the viewer sees & feels:** Built like an anime/arcade battle where Phone Distractions (doomscrolling, infinite feed monsters) challenge the cute T1 mascot. The mascot uses T1's "Deep Focus Armor" and "Strict Lock" to parry distractions, converting screen time into focus XP. It feels hyper-energetic, fun, and turns boring self-control into an exciting game.
- **First 2 seconds:** A fighting-game health bar depleting with icons of Instagram and TikTok attacking, mascot panting on low health until an alert screams *"CHALLENGER: PROCRASTINATION"*.
- **Beats:** Hook (Distraction boss attacks) → Tension (Focus bar running out) → Reveal ("T1: Turn focus into a fight") → Proof (Focus timer powers up mascot, blocking distractions with shields) → Payoff (Boss KO'd, +500 Focus XP, leveled up) → End Card ("Master your mind with T1").
- **Look:** Cyberpunk arcade neon, high-contrast dark palette with electric purple and cyan glow, pixel-art XP meters paired with smooth 3D mascot.
- **Sound:** 8-bit arcade alert, energetic synth-rock beat drop, shield deflection sound effects, victory fanfare.

---

### Storyline 3: "A Day in Split Screen: With vs Without T1"
- **Device & Form:** Life before and after / Split screen to one
- **What the viewer sees & feels:** The reel splits down the middle: Left side is "Without T1" (mascot procrastinating, phone checking every 30 seconds, stress accumulating, unread books), Right side is "With T1" (mascot calmly ticking off deep work sprints, drinking coffee, glowing with steady energy). At 15 seconds, the left side dissolves into the right side as T1 unites the day in complete focus. The viewer feels a sudden sense of relief and urgency to install the app.
- **First 2 seconds:** Screen splits vertically down the center: Left shows mascot zoning out with phone dropping on face; Right shows mascot smiling, crushing a focus sprint with a glowing timer at `25:00`.
- **Beats:** Hook (Split reality at 9:00 AM) → Tension (Left falls behind while Right powers through) → Reveal (T1: The difference is your system) → Proof (Left side gets locked into T1, transforming into the productive mascot) → Payoff (United full-screen mascot enjoying guilt-free evening peace) → End Card ("Take control of your day. T1.").
- **Look:** Split two-tone: muted desaturated grey-purple on the left vs vibrant glowing violet and warm amber on the right.
- **Sound:** Chaotic overlapping notification dings on the left panning to clean, crisp rhythmic study beat on the right, ending in a harmonious swell.
