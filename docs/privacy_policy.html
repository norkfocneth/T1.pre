T1. TOP 1%

Privacy & Data Policy

Effective Date: August 24, 2026 • Developer: Focneth • Package: com.focneth.t1

🛡️ 1. Core Privacy Philosophy

At T1: Top 1% (developed by Focneth), your digital focus and usage habits belong solely to you. We do not sell your personal data, we do not run third-party advertising SDKs, and we strictly enforce a Local-First Privacy Architecture.

✓ All raw application telemetry, usage durations, and screen time events are analyzed locally on your device and are never sent to external servers.

📱 2. On-Device Local Processing

T1 requests specific Android permissions solely to compute your personal focus baselines and relative trends:

A. Android Usage Statistics (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

  • What is accessed: Foreground application durations, category classification (Productivity, Social, Entertainment, Education), unlock counts, and session metrics.
  • Why it is needed: To calculate your 7-day relative baseline and intra-day focus score differentials (e.g. Time Saved vs Yesterday).
  • Guarantee: We NEVER read personal messages, keystrokes, browsing history, photos, or file contents.

B. Installed Apps Metadata (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES)

  • What is accessed: Package metadata to automatically classify apps into behavioral categories.
  • Guarantee: Your list of installed applications is never transmitted or saved on remote servers.

☁️ 3. Cloud Synchronized Information

To enable account persistence and global league standings, we synchronize the minimum necessary aggregated data via encrypted Supabase PostgreSQL (TLS 1.3 in-transit, AES-256 at-rest):

  • Account Identity: Google OAuth user ID, registered email, and optional display name.
  • Aggregated Scores: Numerical Focus Score (0–99), daily focus streak, T1 Token balance, and total completed focus minutes.
  • Primary Goal Track: Selected focus arena (Founder / Productivity, Aukaat / Student, Social Control).

🔒 Stealth Privacy Mode: You can enable Stealth Mode at any time in Profile Settings to completely mask your username as "Anonymous / Shadow" on all public leaderboards.

🚫 4. What We Never Do

  • Zero Advertisements: We do not integrate Google AdMob, Unity, Meta, or any ad networks.
  • Zero Data Selling: We will never sell, lease, or monetize your data to data brokers or third parties.
  • Zero Keystroke / Screen Recording: No background audio, camera, or screen capturing is ever executed.

🗑️ 5. Instant Account Deletion (Right to Erasure)

In full compliance with Google Play's 2023 Account Deletion Policy and global privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA/DPDP), you can permanently delete your entire account and all associated cloud data directly from the app:

📍 Path in App: Profile → Settings (⚙️) → Danger Zone → Delete Account (instant 0-day deletion of all profiles, focus scores, tokens, and records).

You may also request account deletion via email by contacting support@focneth.com.
