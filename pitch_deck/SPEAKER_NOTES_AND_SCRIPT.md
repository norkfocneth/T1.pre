## 🎙️ Slide-by-Slide Speaking Script

### 🟢 Slide 1: Title & Hook (The Big Vision)
- **Visual**: Glowing T1 branding, minimalist dark theme, 3 core architecture stats.
- **Presenter Script**:
  > *"Good morning / afternoon everyone. Today, artificial intelligence produces infinite content, algorithms are engineered to capture human dopamine, and attention spans are at an all-time low.
  > In this world, **unbroken human deep focus is the single rarest and most valuable commodity on the planet.**
  > 
  > We are building **T1: Top 1%**. It is the world’s first **Proof-of-Focus Engine** designed for students, founders, and ambitious builders. We turn raw digital self-discipline into a verifiable, high-stakes token economy."*

---

### 🔴 Slide 2: The Problem (The Trillion-Dollar Attention Trap)
- **Visual**: 3 Glass cards (Cognitive Decay, Blockers Don't Work, Zero Proof of Work).
- **Presenter Script**:
  > *"Let’s talk about the real problem. The average ambitious student or young professional unlocks their phone 90 to 120 times a day. Every notification shatters deep work.
  > 
  > But here is why current solutions fail: **App blockers treat humans like toddlers.** 
  > They put up artificial locks. What happens? In 48 hours, when a user urgently needs their phone, friction causes frustration, and 87% of users disable or uninstall the blocker.
  > 
  > More importantly: **There is zero proof-of-work for focus.** You can go to the gym and get muscle, you can code and push to GitHub, but when you spend 6 hours in deep focus, there is zero verifiable status or proof."*

---

### 🔵 Slide 3: The Paradigm Shift (Not Blocking — Upgrading)
- **Visual**: Side-by-side comparison of Old Blocker Model vs T1 Relative Model.
- **Presenter Script**:
  > *"This is T1’s fundamental breakthrough. **T1 does not block your phone.**
  > 
  > Instead, T1 is an **unfiltered mirror of reality** answering one simple question: *'Are you upgrading compared to yesterday?'*
  > 
  > Instead of arbitrary 2-hour limits, T1 establishes a **7-Day Dynamic Behavioral Baseline**. If you typically spend 4 hours on distractions and today you cut it to 2.5 hours, you have saved 1.5 hours of life. You win. You upgrade your percentile score and you earn T1 Tokens."*

---

### 🟣 Slide 4: Technology & Architecture (7-Day Baseline Engine)
- **Visual**: 3-step architecture pipeline + Interactive Token Flow diagram.
- **Presenter Script**:
  > *"How does the engine work? 
  > 1. **Zero-Friction, Local Telemetry**: T1 ingests foreground usage events through the Android UsageStats API. Raw logs, private messages, and package details stay 100% on the device.
  > 2. **Intra-Day Normalization**: Our proprietary baseline engine anchors calculations to the start-of-day baseline, preventing compound score runaway and giving authentic intra-day feedback.
  > 3. **Percentile Rank Engine**: Translates your discipline into a 0 to 99 Focus Score benchmarked against peers in real time."*
