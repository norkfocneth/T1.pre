T1. · HOW IT WORKS
05

A simple, three-step methodology.

Designed around cognitive psychology principles to foster long-term behavioral change without overwhelming friction.

PHASE 01
UNDERSTAND

T1 monitors background interaction patterns using Android's native UsageStats API without accessing personal messages, browsing content, or credentials.

Result: Uncovers exact times of involuntary attention leakage throughout the day.
PHASE 02
INTERVENE

At the moment of distraction, T1 presents calm, timely micro-interventions or launches a focused peer session to break compulsive phone unlock loops.

Result: Replaces automated dopamine scrolling with deliberate conscious choice.
PHASE 03
IMPROVE

Focus time is recognized and recorded. Users earn token benchmarks, track weekly consistency, and observe measurable improvements in continuous work blocks.

Result: Builds self-reinforcing daily momentum that outlasts temporary motivation.
